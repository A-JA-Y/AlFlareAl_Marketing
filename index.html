Our Founder

Meet the Spark Behind FlareAI

👨‍💻
Marcus Rivera
Founder & CEO

Marcus Rivera is a serial entrepreneur and digital marketing veteran who spent 15 years helping small businesses compete against corporations with deep pockets. After building and selling two marketing agencies, Marcus realized the same playbook he used for Fortune 500 clients could be automated and made accessible to every business.

He holds a marketing degree from NYU and spent six years as Head of Growth at two VC-backed startups, developing proprietary frameworks for content-driven customer acquisition. His frustration with watching talented small business owners fail not due to bad products, but due to poor marketing visibility, became his driving force.

He built FlareAI to democratize sophisticated marketing — giving every small business an AI-powered marketing department for less than the cost of a single freelancer hour. He's a sought-after speaker at SaaStr and Growth Summit, and an advisor to 12 early-stage startups.
